# EPCW Interactive updates

## Latest: version 2.2.0

[Download the 2.2.0 installer](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/goncalvesmathias123-art/mc-stuff/v2.2.0/installers/EPCW-Interactive-2.2.0-Setup.exe).

Use **Check for updates → Download → Restart & install**. Close both games before installation. Older-host launchers require one manual migration installation.

Water contact now starts immediate pursuit, with no timed hold before a contact grab. Targeting updates immediately and close charges accelerate quickly. The creature still stops against the player. Pebbles remain the intentional distraction mechanic.

The view extends through the loaded room instead of cutting scenery off at 40 metres. Course/room distance cutoffs are removed; the current course and next room already exist before movement. Interior ambient fill preserves tile/creature detail beneath the ceiling shadows.

Repeated water jumps no longer replay the loud entry splash. Initial water entry and footsteps are softer; pebble impacts have a separate quiet plop.

Keeps 100 levels, tiny/raised pads, marked water gaps, 20-pebble inventory/refills, gentler paths, hammer and separated spinning sweeper per level, reference-style lighting and green water, saves, serif pause menu, crows, connected tunnels and two-second catch static. Original Parkour Paridise 2 and launcher controls/mappings remain.

Validation: 7,251 core checks, instant contact, repeated water/dry jump sound events, actual rendering past the former 40 m boundary, physical contact and pebble-assisted crossing tests, 15 launcher tests, browser controls and actual exported-game integration. Final exported room lighting is inspected. Native Windows execution, human balancing and performance on the user's PC remain untested.

Updates retain the existing signing key. The launcher verifies signature, installer size and SHA-256. No private key or user data is published. Liberation Serif is redistributed with its SIL Open Font License.
